About these numbers. The 2,138 total, the <2 hr count (674), and the ≥2 hr count (747) are exact figures from the Intercom API for the 60-day window of Mar 23 – May 22, 2026. The analyzed sample is 2,136 of those 2,138 conversations (99.9% coverage), so per-category counts and close times are near-exact rather than projected. Empty chat-widget opens — people who clicked the chat bubble but never sent a message — made up 37% of all records and have been excluded from the category analysis since they aren't real support requests. The remaining 717 conversations are still open (never reached a first close).
